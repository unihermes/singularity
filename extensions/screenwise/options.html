Screenwise

This browser keeps every page fullscreen inside its window, so Full screen can't fill the screen. Open about:config and set full-screen-api.ignore-widgets to false.

Modes

In window
The video or page fills the browser window; tabs and toolbars stay visible.
Full screen
The page fills the whole screen, as browsers normally do.
Ask
Choose each time a page goes fullscreen, with an option to remember it.

Hold Shift while going fullscreen to be asked just that once, whatever the site is set to.
